Level 1 vs. Level 2 Charging

Choosing the right charger depends on battery capacity, daily mileage, and home electrical wiring.

Level 1 (Standard Outlet)

Uses a standard wall outlet. Great choice for Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs) or drivers with shorter daily commutes who can charge overnight.

Level 2 (Wallbox / High Power)

Requires a 240V/400V dedicated circuit or Wallbox. Delivers significantly faster charging and is ideal for Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) with larger battery packs.
