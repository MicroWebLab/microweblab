Level 1 or Level 2?

The right choice depends on your vehicle, daily driving, available electrical service and how quickly you need to recharge.

Level 1

Uses a standard 120V household outlet. It can work for drivers with relatively low daily mileage or some plug-in hybrids, but charging is slower.

Level 2

Uses 240V electrical service and provides substantially faster home charging. Installation requirements vary by home.
