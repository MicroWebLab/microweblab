Practical Guides

MicroWebLab is not only a collection of buttons and calculators. The applications also include educational material that explains how their calculations, estimates and external services should be interpreted.

Understanding EV Charging

Charging time depends on more than battery size. The maximum charging rate of the vehicle, charger power, battery state of charge, temperature and charging losses can all influence the actual result.

A simple estimate starts by comparing the amount of energy that must be added to the battery with the effective charging power. Real charging is rarely perfectly constant, so calculator results should be treated as planning estimates rather than guaranteed charging times.

Calculating Resale Profit

A selling price alone does not tell you whether a resale was profitable. A useful calculation should consider the purchase cost, marketplace fees, payment fees, shipping, packaging and any other direct expense associated with the sale.

Profit margin is also different from simple profit. Comparing both values makes it easier to evaluate whether the time and money invested in an item make sense.

Read the Resell Knowledge Center →

Indicative Prices vs. Live Prices

An indicative estimate is a reference value generated from a defined method or built-in data. It should never be presented as if it were a current listing fetched directly from a marketplace when no live marketplace API is being used.

MicroWebLab therefore distinguishes its locally calculated estimates from live offers. When a current purchasing or selling decision matters, the original marketplace remains the source that should be checked.

How TuneWiseALL Uses YouTube

TuneWiseALL uses YouTube services to locate publicly available videos and reproduce them through a visible embedded YouTube player. The application does not provide its own copy of the audio or video file.

Features such as favorites and playlists organize references inside the application, while the actual media remains provided by YouTube. Availability can therefore change when a video is removed, restricted or made unavailable by its provider.

What Makes These Tools Different?

Each MicroWebLab application is built around a clearly defined task instead of trying to become a general-purpose portal.

Practical First

MicroWebLab focuses on common problems that can be improved with a lightweight browser tool: calculating charging time, estimating a resale margin, organizing marketplace research or managing a personal music queue.

The goal is to make useful functions immediately available without forcing users through unnecessary account creation or complicated setup.

Clear About Data

Where a value is an estimate, we identify it as an estimate. Where content comes from an external service such as YouTube or a marketplace, that relationship is explained rather than presenting third-party information as MicroWebLab's own data.

This distinction is especially important for changing information such as product availability, commercial prices and online media content.

Browser-Based

The current MicroWebLab projects are designed primarily as browser-based applications. Many personal settings such as favorites or inventories are stored locally in the visitor's browser rather than requiring a MicroWebLab user account.

Individual applications explain their own storage and third-party integrations in more detail within their privacy information.

Continuously Improved

Applications are updated when practical issues are found during real use. Improvements may include clearer explanations, compatibility adjustments, accessibility improvements or new functionality.

We aim to keep each tool understandable and useful rather than adding features simply to make an application appear more complex.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about MicroWebLab and how the applications work.

Are MicroWebLab tools free to use?

The current web versions can be opened directly in a browser. Future functionality, monetization or platform versions may change, and any important changes will be disclosed where relevant.

Does MicroWebLab sell products?

No. EVChargeWiseALL and ResellWiseALL can link users to independent external marketplaces and services, but MicroWebLab does not sell the products shown on those external platforms.

Are displayed estimates guaranteed prices?

No. Locally generated indicative estimates are references only and should not be interpreted as guaranteed current market prices, offers or valuations. Always check the original marketplace or provider before making a financial decision.

Does TuneWiseALL host music?

No. TuneWiseALL uses YouTube services and a visible embedded YouTube player. MicroWebLab does not host or distribute the underlying audio or video files.

Do I need a MicroWebLab account?

No user account is currently required for the three main web applications. Some preferences and user-created information may be stored locally in the visitor's browser.

Can information on external websites change?

Yes. Prices, listings, product availability, videos and other third-party information can change at any time. External services remain responsible for the information displayed on their own websites and platforms.
