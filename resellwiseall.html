Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

No Account Required

ResellWiseALL does not require registration or login. All core features — marketplace search, the Profit Calculator, the ROI Calculator, Inventory, AI Analysis and Analytics/Insights — work without creating an account.

What We Store Locally

ResellWiseALL stores data locally in your browser using LocalStorage — specifically, the favorite (saved) items and Inventory entries you explicitly add. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties. The ROI Calculator runs entirely in your browser and does not persist its input values — a calculation is cleared on page refresh unless you manually save the item to Inventory.

Product and Pricing Information

Product, pricing and offer information shown in the app is fetched or estimated from external e-commerce sources. We do not generate this data ourselves — we only display it to help you compare and analyze. The "ResellWise Insights" section displays estimated/demo figures computed from your local Inventory and from estimated search results, and is clearly labeled as such — it is not verified real market data.

ResellWiseALL uses Google Analytics (via Google's gtag.js) to collect anonymized usage data such as visit counts, feature usage and general technical/device information. This data is not used to identify you individually for marketing purposes.

ResellWiseALL does not sell personal data to third parties.

Because ResellWiseALL does not use accounts or a backend database, most data protection rights apply directly to the data stored in your own browser:
